What it takes

Five qualities. Your week covers four.

HYROX has no technical lifts and no gymnastics. Every movement is one you already know: run, push, pull, carry, lunge, squat and throw. The race tests five qualities, and this is how a week of gym, yoga, a run or walk, and tennis lines up against them.

Relay: who does what

Popular tips from relay guides, not rules: the team decides who does which legs, back to back or split up.

  1. 1SkiErgA
  2. 2Sled pushB
  3. 3Sled pullB
  4. 4Burpee jumpsC
  5. 5RowA
  6. 6Farmers carryD
  7. 7LungesD
  8. 8Wall ballsC

A · Beginner

  • 1SkiErg 1,000 m
  • 5Row 1,000 m

Two machines, no weights: you set the pace and can pause (stay seated on the rower), with a long rest in between.

B · Strongest

  • 2Sled push 50 m
  • 3Sled pull 50 m

The sleds only move if you're strong enough. Tough back to back, so two strong people can split them.

C · Fittest

  • 4Burpee broad jumps 80 m
  • 8100 wall balls

Two of the most tiring stations. The other three cheer you through all 100 wall balls, then all four cross the line together.

D · All-rounder

  • 6Farmers carry 200 m
  • 7Sandbag lunges 100 m

Tests your grip, then your legs.

Ready for relay if you can…

  1. Jog 1 km, twice. Walk breaks are fine.
  2. SkiErg (a standing ski-pull machine) 1,000 m at your own pace.
  3. Row 1,000 m at your own pace.

What doesn't get easier: the weights are the same as solo, and every station must be finished in full or the team is disqualified. Teams are 4 women, 4 men, or 2 and 2.

Same week, re-tilted

Your next 8 weeks

Keep the five sessions you already do. Change what goes into the two gym days, make the run a real run, and add one short run.

Gym →

Strength and wall balls

Squats or leg press, deadlifts, rows, farmers carries. Finish with 50 wall balls.

Gym →

HYROX circuit

4 to 6 rounds of a 1 km run plus one station: 500 m row, 20 wall balls, 20 lunges or a 100 m carry.

Run or walk →

Easy run

30 to 45 minutes at a pace where you can still talk. Walk breaks are fine.

Yoga

Keep it

Hips, hamstrings and breathing. It doubles as your recovery day.

Tennis

Keep it

It is already an interval session.

New

Second run

20 to 30 minutes easy, or 4 to 6 × 1 km at a steady pace with 90 seconds of walking between.

  1. Weeks 1–3

    BaseAdd the second run. Learn wall balls, lunges and the rower. Circuits at 4 rounds.

  2. Weeks 4–6

    BuildCircuits up to 6 rounds. Run the 1 km pieces at the pace you'd race. One half-race simulation.

  3. Week 7

    RehearseA full or three-quarter simulation, ideally with your race partner.

  4. Week 8

    TaperCut training volume by about 40%. Keep a little speed. Sleep more.
